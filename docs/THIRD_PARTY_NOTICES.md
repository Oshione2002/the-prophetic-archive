# Third-party notices

The application bundles **Inter** and **Source Serif 4** for offline typography.
Both typefaces are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The complete
license texts are checked in beside the font files:

- `assets/fonts/OFL-Inter.txt`
- `assets/fonts/OFL-SourceSerif4.md`

Flutter and all Dart/Worker dependencies retain their respective upstream
licenses. A production distributor must generate and review the complete
dependency notice bundle before release. Archive-content copyright and
publisher attribution are separate release inputs and are intentionally not
inferred by this repository.
